# Tactical-Task Decision — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams pick one doctrinal task for the port landing. Source: student-deck scenario (amphibious entry, partial compromise of port logistics and maritime systems, existing logical access, doctrinal task required). The content guide's shorter workstation-and-wipe case is the same decision problem. Acceptable tasks below are generated from that scenario because the deck does not fill in a single verb.

## Expected product

One task, its purpose, why the nearest alternatives lose, and the technical actions that execute the task.

## Acceptable answers

- Strong: Interdict — interrupt adversary use of the port network in order to preserve the landing, without claiming seizure of the port.
- Strong: Isolate or Contain — separate the compromised maritime and logistics systems so the landing force is not depending on them.
- Acceptable: Deny or Degrade, if the purpose still protects the landing and the team says what is denied.
- Weak but discussable: Block, if they account for a dormant wipe or other reaction and do not treat the block as the task name "we will block the IP."
- Do not accept: "patch it," "run an exploit," or Destroy, when destruction risks the port the landing needs.

Technical actions are acceptable when they serve the chosen task (sinkhole or filter adversary command paths, segment maritime systems, preserve logs). They are not acceptable as a substitute for the task.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: one doctrinal task, a purpose, a rejected alternative, and technical actions that match the task.
- Do not accept: a tool plan with no task.
