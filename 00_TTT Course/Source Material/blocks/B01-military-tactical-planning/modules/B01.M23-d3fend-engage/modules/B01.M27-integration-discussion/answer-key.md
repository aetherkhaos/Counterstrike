# Integration Discussion — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams say when an engagement stops being only defense. Source: parent content guide slide 13 and the student-deck headings for intelligence requirements, staff integration, and ownership transition.

## Expected product

For one option: the intelligence requirement, who deconflicts intelligence staff and cyber staff, and the condition that moves ownership.

## Acceptable answers

- The requirement names a fact the engagement must reveal. "More intel" fails.
- Deconfliction names who tasks, who collects, and who reports.
- The ownership trigger is a condition, not a vibe. Examples that earn credit: a lure is used; collection would leave the defended network; the commander's question changes from "stop this" to "what else do they run."
- Who owns the data, who exploits it, and what trigger changes ownership are the three required questions.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: a requirement, a staff handoff, and a trigger.
- Do not accept: an engagement plan with no owner.
