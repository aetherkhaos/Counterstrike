# 2X/39 Integration

This Module was previously an embedded exercise in [B01.M23 MITRE D3FEND and ENGAGE](../B01.M23-d3fend-engage/content-guide.md). Complete it in sequence immediately after that lecture.

### Slide 13: The 2X/39 Conversation

- **Priority intelligence requirements:** what must the engagement reveal?
- **Staff integration:** how will intelligence and cyber staffs deconflict tasking, collection, and reporting?
- **Ownership transition:** what exact condition moves activity from CPT-owned defense to intelligence-owned collection?
- For every option ask:
  - Who owns the data?
  - Who exploits and reports it?
  - What trigger changes ownership?

**Instructor Notes:**

- **Image Prompt:** No image required for this slide.
- **Say:** "An ENGAGE action can begin as defense and become a collection opportunity. Define the intelligence requirement, staff relationship, and ownership-transition trigger before execution."
- **Show:** The three integration headings and ownership questions.
- **Do:** Open discussion, timebox **15 minutes**. Capture the deconfliction questions students raise on the whiteboard — these become RFIs for the home-unit intel shop.
