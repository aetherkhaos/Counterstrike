# APT SALUTE — Student Brief

This activity is the extracted in-lecture drill from [Military Tactical Planning](../../content-guide.md). Complete it in sequence with that lecture. Do not treat this folder as a second slide deck.

## Objective
Complete the **APT SALUTE** drill during the parent lecture session. Use the
hour for extraction and a five-minute back-brief, not for reading a report
for the first time.

## Context
Parent lecture: Military Tactical Planning. Slide and instructor notes stay in the parent lecture.

Before day 1 you already read **both** required ESET reports (InvisiMole and
Gamaredon / FSB Center 18). Obtain the issued PDFs from the syllabus

## Tasks
1. Follow the instructor through the parent lecture until this drill.
2. Produce a SALUTE from the assigned in-class report and back-brief it.
3. Keep that product for the rest of this lecture session.
