## 1. Administrative and metadata

### 1.1 Lesson title

Planning Group Assessment

### 1.2 Module length

345 minutes (5.75 hours)

Schedule slot is 345 minutes, the full training day. Student brief 6-7 hours plus two briefs is that same window.

### 1.3 Terminal and enabling learning objectives

- **TLO-1:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will possess the ability to conduct military tactical planning in the context of DCO-RA
- **ELO-10:** Given a scenario and access to resources, conduct a METT-TC analysis and document findings that support the tactical plan.
- **ELO-11:** Given adversary information, conduct a DRAW-D analysis and document capabilities, limitations, and likely actions on contact.
- **ELO-12:** Given an operational environment, determine an adversary’s COG.
- **ELO-13:** Given an operational environment, determine an adversary’s critical vulnerabilities.
- **ELO-14:** Given mapped adversary activity and access to resources, determine the enemy's most likely course of action (EMLCOA).
- **ELO-15:** Given an operational environment, create an exploitation plan.
- **ELO-16:** Given an operational environment and completed mission analysis, develop a scheme of maneuver, fire support plan, and organic asset tasks.
- **ELO-17:** Given an operational environment and mapped adversary activity, determine and explain the adversary's mission.
- **ELO-18:** Given an operational environment, conduct OCOKA-W analysis from friendly and enemy perspectives.

### 4.3 Lesson summary

This assessment validates the Week 1 outcome: students can convert scenario evidence and doctrine into a disciplined tactical plan. It requires them to move from METT-TC through enemy analysis, terrain analysis, EMLCOA, CG/CV, exploitation planning, framework mapping, CAD, SOM, FSP, and tasks while producing a commander-ready handoff product.

### 4.4 Questions and check on learning

- **Ask (Q):** What would the enemy likely do if the first recommended action became visible?
- **Expected Response (A):** Shift infrastructure, reattempt access, accelerate collection, or reinforce through alternate paths — drawn from DRAW-D / EMLCOA, not from a generic "they leave."
- **Ask (Q):** What authority is required before the team acts?
- **Expected Response (A):** The authority named in the option statement; teams must not imply live disruptive action during the assessment.
