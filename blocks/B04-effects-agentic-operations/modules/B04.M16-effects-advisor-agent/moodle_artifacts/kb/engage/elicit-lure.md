# ENGAGE: Elicit — Lure

Lure activities plant or expose a controlled artifact so the adversary reveals
intent, tooling, or follow-on collection. They pair with reconnoiter when
evidence is incomplete.

When classification is disputed or `needs_human_review` is true, a lure or
canary is often a better next step than deny/degrade/disrupt/destroy. The
purpose is to close an RFI, not to burn access.

Do not recommend a destructive effect solely on volume. If two models disagreed
on classification, elicit more evidence first.
