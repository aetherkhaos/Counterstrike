# Capstone — Instructor Rubric

**Assessment:** B05.M01 4-phase capstone  
**Use:** Instructor scoring of products, briefs, Q&A, and notes  
**Points:** Poor = 1, Marginal = 2, Acceptable = 3, Exemplary = 4 per row  
**Crosswalk:** Rows map to validation criteria in [CounterStrike_Capstone_Instructor_Guide.md](CounterStrike_Capstone_Instructor_Guide.md) and deliverables in [Capstone_student_brief.md](Capstone_student_brief.md).

Student-facing note: performance descriptors only — no hidden solution content.

## Phase 1 — Recon and planning

| Instructor question | Poor (1 pt) | Marginal (2 pts) | Acceptable (3 pts) | Exemplary (4 pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1-1 (Weight: recon). Did the student identify greyspace infrastructure and avenues of approach from intel plus active recon, without attempting access?** | No usable recon, or access attempted in this phase. Score: 1 | Partial scan/intel with weak terrain picture. Score: 2 | Redirector/collector/edge picture plus service/version evidence. Score: 3 | Clear topology, scanning discipline, and lowest-risk access options for the commander. Score: 4 |
| **P1-2 (Weight: planning). Did the student produce METT-TC (mission/enemy/terrain), EMLCOA, CG/CV, exploitation plan, SOM, and a D3FEND CAD?** | Products missing or generic. Score: 1 | Partial packet; CAD or CG/CV thin. Score: 2 | Required products present and usable. Score: 3 | Evidence-grounded packet plus D3FEND/ENGAGE-derived bluespace options. Score: 4 |

## Phase 2 — Access and survey

| Instructor question | Poor (1 pt) | Marginal (2 pts) | Acceptable (3 pts) | Exemplary (4 pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P2-1 (Weight: access). Did the student achieve and maintain initial greyspace access with observable evidence?** | No access evidence. Score: 1 | Transient access; not maintained or unexplained. Score: 2 | Stable access with proof of foothold. Score: 3 | Stable access plus documented reentry for later handoff. Score: 4 |
| **P2-2 (Weight: survey). Did the student produce a 4-bucket situational-awareness package (technical, behavioral, attribution/tracking, expansion leads)?** | Survey missing. Score: 1 | Ad-hoc notes; buckets incomplete. Score: 2 | All four buckets present and usable. Score: 3 | Thorough survey that clearly feeds D4M or handoff decisions. Score: 4 |

## Phase 3 — D4M, red-net recon, or handoff

| Instructor question | Poor (1 pt) | Marginal (2 pts) | Acceptable (3 pts) | Exemplary (4 pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P3-1 (Weight: effects). Did the student execute a D4M action on the redirector with operational thought (collection support or capability degrade)?** | No effect, or destructive/out-of-scope action. Score: 1 | Effect claimed without operational rationale. Score: 2 | Bounded D4M with clear purpose. Score: 3 | Creative, reversible-enough D4M tied to CG/CV and collection value. Score: 4 |
| **P3-2 (Weight: decision). Did the student either push a justified redspace attempt or complete a 7-section Access Guide handoff by the phase stop?** | Neither redspace progress nor a usable handoff. Score: 1 | Incomplete guide or unjustified push. Score: 2 | Clear decision: access guide complete **or** redspace attempt documented. Score: 3 | Decision is risk-justified; handoff package or redspace recon is partner-ready. Score: 4 |

## Phase 4 — Redspace and hot wash

| Instructor question | Poor (1 pt) | Marginal (2 pts) | Acceptable (3 pts) | Exemplary (4 pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P4-1 (Weight: redspace recon). Did the student reconnoiter redspace terrain (C2 minimum) and brief exfil/C2 workflow?** | No redspace recon. Score: 1 | Host access without workflow understanding. Score: 2 | C2 surveyed; workflow described. Score: 3 | C2 plus stretch Operator Box or equivalent depth; commander-ready D4M options. Score: 4 |
| **P4-2 (Weight: hot wash). Did the student verbally explain actions, methods, and motives across all phases with notes to back claims?** | Brief missing or unsupported. Score: 1 | Tool dump without decisions. Score: 2 | Coherent phase-by-phase account. Score: 3 | Decision-quality brief: why D4M/handoff choices, what to do next. Score: 4 |

## Scoring notes

- Differentiated path: students who hand off in Phase 3 are not penalized on P4-1 for lacking self-gained redspace access if they use instructor-returned access and still survey.
- Effects on red-net require instructor approval (instructor guide ROE). Out-of-scope actions fail the effects rows.
