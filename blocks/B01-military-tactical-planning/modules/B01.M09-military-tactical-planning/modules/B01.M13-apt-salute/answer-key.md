# APT SALUTE — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Each team writes a SALUTE from an APT report and briefs it. Source: content guide slides 14, 15, and 29, plus the student-deck SALUTE definitions and the FSB Center 18 toolchain slide.

## Expected product

Six SALUTE lines, the RFIs the report cannot answer, and a two-minute brief.

## Acceptable answers

For the FSB Center 18 / Gamaredon report, the worked example is the reference, not a script to copy:

- Size: FSB-resourced. Exact manpower is an RFI.
- Activity: cyberespionage and rapid document collection. Initial access uses spearphishing with LNK or HTA attachments and weaponized removable media. Collection includes documents, browser credentials, cookies, and Signal or Telegram data. Persistence uses weaponized templates and scheduled tasks.
- Location: occupied Crimea physically; global infrastructure and Ukrainian networks logically.
- Unit: FSB Center 18, Military Unit 64829. Industry cluster name Gamaredon is not the planning unit.
- Time: ongoing.
- Equipment: obfuscated scripts, droppers, stealers, backdoors, and SOCKS proxies.

Other assigned reports: accept lines tied to that report. Gaps become RFIs. "Tell me more about the adversary" is not an RFI.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: six lines, evidence versus RFI marked, unit stated as an organization rather than only a cluster nickname.
- Do not accept: a technique list with no SALUTE letters.
