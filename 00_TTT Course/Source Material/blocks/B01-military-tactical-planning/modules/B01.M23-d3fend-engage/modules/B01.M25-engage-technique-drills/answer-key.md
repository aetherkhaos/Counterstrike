# ENGAGE Technique Drills — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Short drills on the deception set. Source: parent content guide slides 5 through 11. The student deck's desktop figure is the picture: lures, pocket litter, information manipulation, introduced vulnerability.

## Expected product

One technique choice against the collection EMLCOA the team already wrote, with the goal it serves.

## Acceptable answers

- EAC0005 Lures is the strongest default against a collection actor: a unique fake credential is a high-confidence expose signal. The deck says the same about unique leaked credentials.
- EAC0011 Pocket Litter supports the story and does not by itself elicit one action. Accept it as a supporting technique, not as the whole plan.
- EAC0015 Information Manipulation and EAC0023 Introduced Vulnerabilities are acceptable when the team names the fiction or the magnet and the goal (usually Elicit).
- Direction is the most active of the Affect approaches. For the exfiltration prompt, accept one prevention option, one direction option, and one disruption option. Throttle or deceive the channel rather than only "block everything" unless they own the risk to friendly traffic.
- Hardware manipulation is the non-negotiable of the two hardware techniques when the room is a shared space with a live microphone or camera.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: a technique, a goal (Expose, Affect, or Elicit), and a reason tied to the actor's mission.
- Do not accept: a technique with no goal.
