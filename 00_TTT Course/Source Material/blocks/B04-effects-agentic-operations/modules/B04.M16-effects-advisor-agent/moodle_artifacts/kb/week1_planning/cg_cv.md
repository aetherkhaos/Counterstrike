# Center of gravity and critical vulnerability (Week 1 extract)

A center of gravity (CG) is the source of power that permits the adversary to
accomplish their objective. For collection operations, the CG is often the
pipeline: ingest endpoint, token, and backing store — not a single packet.

A critical vulnerability (CV) is the characteristic of that CG that makes an
effect feasible. An unauthenticated ingest route, a revocable token, or a
single-homed C2 IP can each be a CV.

Name both CG and CV in the recommendation. An effect without a CV is a guess.
