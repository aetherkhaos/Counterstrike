### Background of the conflict
The roots of the Varensk–Lunovia conflict go back decades:

- After the collapse of an older regional empire, Lunovia gained independence and began aligning with Western economic and security frameworks.
- Varensk’s leadership views this shift as a strategic threat and a betrayal of shared history.
- Lunovia accuses Varensk of attempting to destabilize its government through cyberattacks, disinformation, and support for separatist groups.
 - Over the past five years, Varensk has increased military exercises near the border and conducted several high‑impact cyber operations against Lunovian energy and telecom networks.
- International observers warn that the situation could escalate into open conflict within the next 3–5 years.
