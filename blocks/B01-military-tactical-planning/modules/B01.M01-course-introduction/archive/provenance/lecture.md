# Welcome to the Counter Strike Operator Course

Congratulations on being in this course. This is the pilot and the first ever USCC ACTEC created course.

## What is DCO-RA?

So what exactly is defensive cyberspace operations response actions? Well, we're going to just pull directly from Joint Pub 3-2 and see what it has to say about it. So the by the book definition of DCO-RA are:

"The form of DCO mission where actions are taken external to the defended network or portion of cyberspace without the permission of the owner of the affected system. DCO-RA missions may rise to the level of use of force, with physical damage or destruction of enemy systems, depending on broader operational context, such as the existence of imminence of open hostilities, the degree of certainty in attribution of the threat, the damage the threat has caused or is expected to cause, and national policy considerations. DCO-RA missions, especially when they occur before and imminent threat has a chance to act, are defending forward in support of the persistent engagement strategic approach."

What are some of the key words or phrases in this paragraph?
- **External to the defended network**
- **without the permission of the owner of the affected system**
- **May rise to the level of use of force**
- **Defending forward**

If you are in this course, you have probably spent the majority of your time looking down and in at blue-space and have been explicitly forbidden from "reaching out and touching" gray or red space.

## What is the purpose of this course?
The purpose of this course is to take individuals who have almost exclusively operated within blue-space and to give them the skills that they need to allow them to operate up and out from blue-space.

## What this course is not

This course is not a hunt course. We are not going to teach you how to hunt. We assume that you already know how to hunt and this course is actually going to start from the position of  the hunt has been successful. We are gonna hit on concepts and content that can be taken back to potentially get better at hunting, but it's not a hunt course.
