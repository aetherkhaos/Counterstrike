# Shadowcat Exploration — Instructor Guide

## Objective and context

Extracted in-lecture drill from [`B01.M28`](../../content-guide.md) Slide 4. Students open D3FEND CAD, load MITRE's Shadowcat example, and look at the graph shape before naming node types. Slide notes stay in the parent content guide; this file is the facilitation SoT for the generated lesson plan.

## Dual opening

### Option 1 (Interactive / Whiteboard / Kinesthetic)

- **Say:** "Everyone open CAD. Load Shadowcat.json. When it loads, do not click anything — just look. I want you to see the shape before we talk about the pieces."
- **Do:** Write on the board: File → Example Graphs → Shadowcat.json. Remind teams the Navigator layer stays closed for this look.
- **Ask (Q):** What product will you have at the end of these 3 minutes?
- **Expected Response (A):** A loaded Shadowcat graph you have looked at, not a graph you built. The mini-build is B01.M30.

### Option 2 (Digital Slide / Virtual / Slide Deck)

- **Say:** "Everyone open CAD. Load Shadowcat.json. When it loads, do not click anything — just look. I want you to see the shape before we talk about the pieces."
- **Show:** Parent Slide 4. Live-open https://d3fend.mitre.org/cad/ and load Shadowcat.json on the projector.
- **Do:** Confirm every station can load the example before silent exploration starts.
- **Ask (Q):** What product will you have at the end of these 3 minutes?
- **Expected Response (A):** A loaded Shadowcat graph you have looked at, not a graph you built. The mini-build is B01.M30.

## Facilitation flow

### Step 1: Open CAD and load Shadowcat (1 min)

- **Image Prompt:** No image required for this lab step.
- **Say:** "Browse to https://d3fend.mitre.org/cad/. Go to File, Example Graphs, and pick Shadowcat.json. Shadowcat is MITRE's canonical teaching example."
- **Show:** Demonstrate the load on the projector. Zoom and pan so the room sees the graph, not a blank canvas.
- **Do:** Troubleshoot stations that cannot load CAD before silent exploration starts.
- **Ask (Q):** Why load an example graph before you build your own?
- **Expected Response (A):** See the shape and typed pieces first. Do not click around yet.

### Step 2: Silent look, then what jumps out (2 min)

- **Image Prompt:** No image required for this lab step.
- **Say:** "When it loads, do not click anything — just look. I want you to see the shape before we name node types."
- **Show:** Projector stays on the loaded Shadowcat graph.
- **Do:** Give the room the remainder of the **3-minute** parent-slide timebox. Ask: "Before we name anything — what jumps out at you?" Accept 2-3 answers. Then return to the parent lecture.
- **Ask (Q):** Before we name anything, what jumps out at you on the Shadowcat graph?
- **Expected Response (A):** Facilitation. Accept 2-3 answers. Then continue parent node-category slides. Nested next: B01.M30 mini-build.

## Lesson summary

- CAD loads example graphs so students see a typed attack-and-defend picture before they build one.
- Shadowcat is the MITRE teaching example; students observe, they do not rebuild it here.
- Next in the parent sequence: node categories, then B01.M30 mini-build from the Navigator intrusion.

## Questions and check on learning

- **Ask (Q):** What did this drill collect?
- **Expected Response (A):** A loaded Shadowcat graph and a first look at its shape, not a student-built CAD JSON.
- **Ask (Q):** What comes next after this look?
- **Expected Response (A):** Parent lecture names Action, Object, State, and Miscellaneous nodes. Then B01.M30 is the mini-build.
