# ENGAGE: Affect — Deceive

Deceive activities shape what the adversary believes about the environment.
They are also the correct framing when the "incident" is a false positive: the
operator elects to disregard the alert and treat the traffic as expected.

If partner-contact notes identify the source as a friendly mirror or sanctioned
telemetry collector, do not recommend deny/degrade against that host. Mark the
incident for cleanup and record why no hostile center of gravity is present.
