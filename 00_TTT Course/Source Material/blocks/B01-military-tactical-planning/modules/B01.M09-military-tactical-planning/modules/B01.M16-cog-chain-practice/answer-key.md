# COG Chain Practice — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams defend one center of gravity with critical capabilities, critical requirements, and a critical vulnerability. Source: content guide slides 25 through 27 and the worked example on slide 33.

## Expected product

One COG, the capabilities and requirements that make it real, one CV, and the evidence chain. A competing COG is in bounds.

## Acceptable answers

- Strong COG: the repeatable speed of collection and exfiltration.
- Also strong, if the evidence is explicit: persistent access itself.
- Critical capabilities: execute quickly, find target documents, keep an outbound path.
- Critical requirements: script-based tooling, common persistence points, and observable process or registry activity.
- Candidate CV: reliance on script-heavy and living-off-the-land behavior that can be baselined and detected.
- Do not accept: "their operators," "their malware," or "Russia" as a COG. Those cannot be targeted from this planning problem.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: a specific COG tied to mission, capability, requirement, and vulnerability.
- Do not accept: a slogan with no evidence chain.
