# Tactical-Task Decision — Student Brief

## Objective

State one doctrinal tactical task for a port landing, justify it against close alternatives, and name the technical actions that would carry it out.

## Context

Friendly forces are preparing an amphibious entry into a contested regional port. Local logistics and maritime systems are partly compromised. Your team already has logical access. The commander will not accept a plan stated as "patch it" or "run an exploit."

## Tasks

1. Pick the single most appropriate tactical task from the task list.
2. Say why the nearest alternatives are weaker.
3. Name the technical actions required to execute the task you chose.

## Deliverables / Evidence

A task, a purpose, a short justification, and the technical actions. One back-brief.

## Constraints

Stay inside the port scenario. Do not invent a second mission.

## Success Criteria

The task is doctrinal, the purpose is clear, and the technical actions support that task rather than replacing it.
