# D3FEND Mapping

This Module was previously an embedded exercise in [B01.M23 MITRE D3FEND and ENGAGE](../B01.M23-d3fend-engage/content-guide.md). Complete it in sequence immediately after that lecture.

### Slide 2: Mapping ATT&CK to D3FEND

- D3FEND is a countermeasure knowledge graph and ontology
- Start with a mapped ATT&CK technique
- Identify applicable D3FEND techniques; do not assume a one-to-one answer
- Check whether each option is feasible in the actual terrain
- Record the expected effect on the adversary COG/CV

**Instructor Notes:**

- **Image Prompt:** Three ATT&CK techniques connected to several candidate D3FEND countermeasures.
- **Say:** "The mapping narrows the defensive option space, but it does not make the decision. Terrain, evidence, mission, and risk determine which countermeasure is viable."
- **Show:** https://d3fend.mitre.org/ and trace one technique to candidate countermeasures.
- **Do:** Teams map three Navigator techniques to D3FEND options. Timebox: **15 minutes**.
