### 1.3 Terminal and enabling learning objectives

- **TLO-1:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will possess the ability to conduct military tactical planning in the context of DCO-RA
- **TLO-2:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will be able to exploit opportunities from a position of advantage in blue space.
- **TLO-3:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will be able deny adversary ability to accomplish their mission in cyberspace.
- **TLO-4:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will be able to rapidly access and effect adversary infrastructure
- **TLO-5:** At the end of the Counterstrike Operator Course, students will be able to use local inference and agentic workflows to support hunting and planning, and counter adversary agentic systems.
- **ELO-10:** Given a scenario and access to resources, conduct a METT-TC analysis and document findings that support the tactical plan.
- **ELO-16:** Given an operational environment and completed mission analysis, develop a scheme of maneuver, fire support plan, and organic asset tasks.
- **ELO-22:** Given threat hunt findings and access to resources, map TTP sequencing onto an ATT&CK Navigator layer with analytical justification.
- **ELO-24:** Given an ATT&CK-mapped intrusion, use D3FEND CAD to develop and graph defensive response options.
- **ELO-38:** Recognize initial access opportunities
- **ELO-74:** Given a constrained host, characterize the environment and complete assigned collection.
- **ELO-82:** Given access to adversary infrastructure, execute a bounded effect while preserving collection and attribution discipline.
