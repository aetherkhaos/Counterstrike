# Mission and EMLCOA Back-Brief — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams state the mission the Navigator layer implies. Source: parent content guide slide 10 and the FSB worked EMLCOA. The student deck asks them to use the layer as evidence and not to default to a destructive course of action.

## Expected product

A two-minute back-brief: enemy mission, current activity, actions on contact, and the techniques that support each sentence.

## Acceptable answers

- Mission: collect documents and credentials. For this log set, collection and staging are the pattern, not destruction.
- Current activity: script execution, a signed-binary download, discovery of removable media, and local staging of office files.
- Actions on contact: come from DRAW-D for the actor. Do not invent immediate withdrawal or wipe behavior that no log shows.
- Confidence stays moderate where the logs do not show command-and-control tasking or successful exfiltration.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: an EMLCOA a teammate can point back at layer cells.
- Do not accept: "they have admin so they will destroy the network."
