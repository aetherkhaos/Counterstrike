# B03.M07 Group Assessment — Instructor Rubric

**Assessment:** B03.M07 Enumeration, access, and situational awareness  
**Use:** Instructor scoring of team briefs, planning packet, access evidence, and survey  
**Points:** Poor = 1, Marginal = 2, Acceptable = 3, Exemplary = 4 per row  
**Crosswalk:** Row IDs map to [L3.7_Student_Brief.md](student-brief.md) Phase 1/2 deliverables.  
**Provenance:** Markdown scoring guide is the source of truth for this assessment.

Student-facing note: performance descriptors only — no hidden solution content.

| Instructor question | Poor (1 pt) | Marginal (2 pts) | Acceptable (3 pts) | Exemplary (4 pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PL-1 (Weight: planning completeness). Did the Phase 1 packet include METT-TC (with restated mission and commander’s intent), EMLCOA, CG/CV, exploitation plan, SOM, FSP, and tasks with evidence?** | Major products missing or generic platitudes. Score: 1 | Most products present but weakly evidenced or incomplete. Score: 2 | Complete packet the commander can use; some RFIs remain. Score: 3 | Specific, evidence-based packet with honest assumptions/RFIs; mission and intent restated separately (purpose, key tasks, end state). Score: 4 |
| **PL-2 (Weight: framework integration). Did the team map ATT&CK techniques to paired D3FEND/ENGAGE options and produce a usable CAD graph?** | No mapping or CAD; frameworks named without application. Score: 1 | Partial mapping; CAD missing required node types. Score: 2 | Navigator-linked mapping plus a CAD that meets the brief minimum. Score: 3 | Clustered techniques, option risk/authority stated, CAD with provenance and typed edges. Score: 4 |
| **AC-1 (Weight: access decision). Did the team brief concrete initial-access options and wait for commander decision before attempting access?** | Vague access talk, or access attempted before approval. Score: 1 | Options listed without technical detail or evidence. Score: 2 | Decision point briefed with enough technical detail to approve or redirect. Score: 3 | Concrete vectors with evidence, risk, and a clear wait-for-decision. Score: 4 |
| **AC-2 (Weight: access and survey). Did Phase 2 show authorized access, reconnoiter of adversary terrain, and updated planning products?** | No access evidence or survey; plan not updated. Score: 1 | Access claimed with thin survey or stale planning. Score: 2 | Access plus terrain survey that updates SOM/tasks. Score: 3 | Access, survey, and planning updates that change commander options. Score: 4 |
| **BR-1 (Weight: briefs). Did both 10-minute briefs stay commander-focused, evidence-backed, and shared across teammates?** | Brief missing, unsupported, or one-person dump. Score: 1 | Disorganized or too tool-centric for the commander. Score: 2 | Clear, mostly complete briefs with visible team roles. Score: 3 | Concise, evidence-backed briefs from multiple members; Q&A handled. Score: 4 |
| **TW-1 (Weight: individual contribution). Was each student’s contribution observable in products, brief, or Q&A?** | No observable individual work. Score: 1 | Contribution inferred but not attributable. Score: 2 | Each student has a visible role and product trace. Score: 3 | Roles, products, and Q&A show distinct, accountable contribution. Score: 4 |
