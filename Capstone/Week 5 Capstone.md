**Duration:** 3.5 Training days **Audience:** CounterStrike Operators (individual)  
**Objective:** Validate your mastery of CounterStrike operations by applying Week 1 tactical planning, Week 2 enumeration, Week 3 adversary infrastructure access, and Week 4 effects tradecraft against the same operational problem.

# Phase 1

**CounterStrike Capstone Assessment**  
**Phase 1 of 4**  
**Classification:** FOUO

---

## Situation

You have been provided the Threat Hunting Report detailing the compromise of a BlueNet workstation (`bluenet-blue05`) by ISB Unit 18 “Amber Tide.” The report outlines observed adversary TTPs, persistence mechanisms, and exfiltration activity directed through greyspace infrastructure.

Your unit has been tasked to begin counterstrike operations against this adversary infrastructure.

---

## Commander’s Intent

**No later than the end of day 1**
reconnoiter enemy greyspace in order to identify avenues of approach. Upon discovery of initial access vectors/opportunities, **be prepared to** provide COAs to the commander to achieve initial access.

---

## Mission / Tasks

Conduct reconnaissance of identified enemy infrastructure in greyspace to develop a clear picture of potential access opportunities and adversary activity.

You are expected to: - Produce planning products to support future decision-making. You will utilize the tactical planning process and create a MITRE CAD diagram of the enemy's intrusion into the network. - Analyze available intelligence (primarily the provided Threat Hunting Report). - Develop an understanding of the adversary’s operational picture in greyspace. - Perform active reconnaissance of enemy infrastructure in greyspace.

---

## Key Goals

By the end of this phase, you should be able to answer: - What infrastructure exists in greyspace and how is it connected? - What potential avenues of approach exist into adversary systems? - What is the adversary’s likely purpose and activity based on available intelligence? - What are the most promising (and lowest risk) options for gaining initial access?

---

## Execution Guidance

- Your primary reference for this phase is the **Threat Hunting Report**.
- Focus on disciplined reconnaissance and information gathering.
- Develop planning products that will support decision-making in later phases.
- Maintain detailed notes of your findings, tools used, and observations. These will be critical during the final hot wash.

**Start Condition:** Receipt of this tasking and the Threat Hunting Report.  
**Stop Condition:** Completion of reconnaissance and delivery of planning products to the commander (or designated instructor).

---

## Important Notes

- Do **not** attempt to gain access during this phase. Your task is reconnaissance and planning only.
- Quality of your analysis and planning products will directly impact your effectiveness in subsequent phases.
- Keep thorough notes throughout this phase.

---

**End State and Deliverables:** - A MITRE D3FEND CAD Diagram that captures enemy MITRE ATT&CK TTPs, artifacts, and corresponding MITRE D3FEND TTPs. - A recommendation to the commander of potential bluespace actions to be taken derived from MITRE D3FEND and MITRE ENGAGE. - A completed METT-TC, EMLCOA, CG/CV/EXP. Plan, and Scheme of Maneuver in the form of a brief to your mission commander. For the METT-TC, only Mission, Enemy, and Terrain and Weather need to be completed.

# Phase 2

**CounterStrike Capstone Assessment**  
**Phase 2 of 4**  
**Classification:** FOUO

---

## Situation

Following successful reconnaissance of greyspace during Phase 1, you now have an understanding of potential access opportunities into adversary infrastructure.

The commander has directed you to exploit identified opportunities and establish a foothold in greyspace.

---

## Commander’s Intent

**No later than the end of day 2**, exploit discovered vulnerability and achieve initial access. Upon gaining initial access to greyspace, reconnoiter terrain in order to provide the commander with COAs to achieve D4M or access into redspace. Be prepared to hand off access to a supporting unit.

---

## Mission / Tasks

Gain initial access into greyspace adversary infrastructure, conduct a thorough survey of the compromised system(s), and develop situational awareness to support follow-on decisions.

You are expected to: - Achieve initial access into greyspace. - Conduct a comprehensive survey of the system(s) you have accessed. - Organize your findings into a usable format for planning and potential handoff. - Begin preparations for possible access transfer to a higher echelon operator.

---

## Key Goals

By the end of this phase, you should be able to answer: - Have I successfully gained and maintained initial access? - What is the current state of the system(s) I have accessed? - What useful information have I gathered that supports D4M planning or further access into redspace? - Am I prepared to hand off this access if directed?

---

## Execution Guidance

- Execute initial access using methods derived from your Phase 1 reconnaissance.
- Once access is achieved, immediately prioritize a thorough host survey.
- Organize your findings clearly. High-quality documentation now will significantly improve your options in later phases.
- Maintain detailed notes of your actions, tools used, and observations. These will be essential during the final hot wash.

**Start Condition:** Completion of Phase 1 planning products and commander approval to proceed.  
**Stop Condition:** Initial access achieved, survey completed, and findings organized for follow-on decision-making (or handoff preparation).

---

## Important Notes

- Focus on achieving stable initial access and conducting a disciplined survey.
- Quality of your documentation and organization of findings will directly affect your performance in Phase 3.
- Keep thorough notes of your process and decisions. You will be expected to explain your actions and rationale during the hot wash.

---

**End State:** You have achieved initial access into greyspace, completed a survey of the compromised system(s), and are prepared to support D4M planning or access handoff.

# Phase 3

**CounterStrike Capstone Assessment**  
**Phase 3 of 4**  
**Classification:** FOUO

---

## Situation

You have established initial access into greyspace and conducted an initial survey of the compromised system(s). The commander now requires you to generate effects and/or prepare for deeper operations into redspace.

---

## Commander’s Intent

Gain access to redspace in order to achieve source range D4M **OR** handoff access if unable. **NLT 1200** be prepared to transfer access if red space access is not gained.

---

## Mission / Tasks

Generate effects against adversary infrastructure in greyspace and/or develop options for access into redspace. If you determine that further access into redspace is not feasible or advisable with your current resources, you are expected to execute a clean and complete handoff of your access to a higher echelon operator.

You are expected to: - Develop and execute D4M options against adversary infrastructure in greyspace. - Conduct reconnaissance of redspace from your current foothold. - Make a deliberate decision on whether to attempt further access into redspace or execute a handoff. - If handing off: Complete all required elements of the access transfer package to a high standard.

---

## Key Goals (From Your Perspective)

By the end of this phase, you should be able to answer: - What D4M options have I developed and executed against greyspace infrastructure? - What have I learned about redspace through reconnaissance from my foothold? - Have I made a clear, justified decision on whether to push into redspace or hand off access? - If handing off, have I produced a complete and professional access transfer package?

---

## Execution Guidance

- Develop D4M options that align with the commander’s intent to affect adversary operations.
- Conduct reconnaissance of redspace infrastructure visible from your current position.
- **Critical Decision Point:** By **NLT 1200**, you must decide whether you will attempt to gain access into redspace or execute a handoff. This decision should be based on your assessment of risk, feasibility, and operational value.
- If you choose to hand off access, you must produce a complete and high-quality access transfer package.

**Start Condition:** Completion of Phase 2 survey and commander approval to proceed.  
**Stop Condition:** D4M effects executed (or prepared), redspace reconnaissance completed, and either redspace access achieved **or** a complete handoff executed by NLT 1200.

---

## Important Notes

- **Handoff Decision (Critical):** You have until **1200** to decide whether to attempt redspace access or transfer your current access to a senior operator. This is a deliberate operational decision.
- If handing off, ensure your access transfer package is complete and professional. The receiving operator will rely on the quality of your documentation.
- The commander expects well-reasoned D4M options. Thoughtful and creative approaches that support operational objectives are valued.
- Maintain detailed notes of your actions, decisions, and rationale. You will be expected to brief these during the hot wash.

---

**End State:** You have either achieved access into redspace or executed a complete handoff, while generating meaningful D4M effects against greyspace infrastructure.
