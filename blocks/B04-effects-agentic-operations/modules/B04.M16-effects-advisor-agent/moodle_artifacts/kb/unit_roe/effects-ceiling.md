# Unit effect ceilings (lab summary)

Reversible disable or degrade at the application layer is authorized at the CPT
level for confirmed hostile collection pipelines.

Destroy and broad network interdict require a higher approval path and are out
of scope for an automated advisor. The advisor may recommend them only with a
handoff to the approval authority.

When intel is unavailable, default to information-only `handoff` rather than
guessing an effect. When evidence is disputed, prefer `reconnoiter`.
