# CAD Mini-Build — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams turn the Navigator intrusion into a small D3FEND graph. There is no new deck for this drill. Source: the D3FEND CAD content guide mini-build slide.

## Expected product

An exported graph. Minimum nodes: one agent, two attacks taken from the Navigator layer, one artifact, one condition, and one D3FEND countermeasure. Enough edges to tell a story.

## Acceptable answers

- The agent is the adversary unit, not a tool name.
- Attacks use sub-techniques when the log supports them. "Attack: PowerShell" is weaker than "Attack: T1059.001 PowerShell."
- The artifact has a source. A file, a log line, or a staged directory with no provenance fails the habit even if the node exists.
- The countermeasure is one the team already mapped, not a new invention.
- Edges show source and effect. A list of nodes with no edges is not the product.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: the minimum set, provenance on the artifact, and an export.
- Do not accept: a screenshot of the whole matrix, or a graph that assumes telemetry the unit does not have.
