# FSB Center 18 Worked Plan — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

This is the instructor worked chain, not the Varensk student practical. Source: content guide slides 29 through 36 and the student-deck fire-support and task tables.

## Expected product

Students can follow the chain and challenge one line. They should not hand this back as their own Varensk plan.

## Acceptable answers

- Mission: deny or degrade FSB Center 18 collection from Network X in order to preserve friendly freedom of maneuver.
- EMLCOA: continuous collection for Russian intelligence requirements; script access, document theft, credential and browser collection, resilient command and control; on contact, alternate channels and reinforcement rather than immediate withdrawal. Confidence is moderate. Support relationships remain RFIs.
- Exploitation plan: shorten detection time with ATT&CK-mapped telemetry, then isolate and scope affected systems.
- Scheme of maneuver: prepare and baseline; detect; isolate and triage; scope and hunt; disseminate. Phase 4 feeds Phase 2.
- Fire support rows from the deck:
  - Confirmed beacon from an isolated host. Organic: block outbound traffic and submit the request. Rear: recon and exploit the node. Effects: degrade, disrupt, exploit.
  - Novel payload URL from memory. Organic: block the download and send the URL. Rear: enumerate the toolkit and neutralize the server. Effects: deny and intelligence gain.
  - Traffic through a known relay. Organic: null-route and submit the request. Rear: partner or authorized action. Effect: disrupt.
- Organic tasks from the deck: mission commander authorizes quarantine and requests and manages risk; threat intel updates TTPs and the EMLCOA; network analyst monitors and blocks local command-and-control addresses; host analyst quarantines and triages.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: a challenge to one DRAW-D or fire-support line that cites evidence or writes an RFI.
- Do not accept: a copy of this example pasted into the Varensk practical.
