A large, authoritarian, resource‑rich state with an assertive foreign policy and a mature offensive cyber program.

### Core identity
Varensk is a continental power of 90 million people, spanning cold northern plains and a long eastern coastline. It is governed by a centralized nationalist regime that blends military influence with state‑controlled industry. Its economy is driven by natural gas, rare‑earth minerals, and heavy manufacturing.
### Geography
Varensk is a vast northern continental state with a cold climate, long winters, and extensive natural resources.
- Capital: Karsograd
- Population: ~90 million
- Terrain: Northern tundra, central plains, eastern coastal marshlands
- Borders: Lunovia (SW), Karsenic Sea (E), three minor neutral states (W/S)
- Key geographic features:
	- The Iron Steppe: A wide, sparsely populated region ideal for armored maneuver warfare.
	- The Voryn Mountains: Natural barrier along the southern border.
	- The Karsenic Coast: Hosts Varensk’s major naval bases and cyber‑intelligence hubs.
- Strategic value:
	- Rich in natural gas, rare earth minerals, and heavy industry.
	- Deepwater ports enabling global naval operations.

### Political Systems
An authoritarian, centralized state with a nationalist ideology.
- Government type: One‑party presidential republic
- Leader: President Viktor Surnov (in power 17 years)
- Power structure:
	- Presidency dominates all branches
	- Security services integrated into governance
	- State‑controlled media and industry
	- Ideology: “Varensker Revivalism”—emphasizes historical unity, cultural dominance, and regional hegemony
- Foreign policy:
	- Reassert control over “historic territories”
	- Undermine democratic neighbors
	- Use cyber operations as a primary tool of influence

### Strategic posture
• 	Pursues regional dominance and views neighboring states as part of its “historic security sphere.”
• 	Maintains a large, well‑funded cyber command integrated with intelligence services.
• 	Uses cyber operations for espionage, political pressure, and infrastructure disruption.
• 	Conducts information operations aimed at shaping narratives in neighboring countries.

### Military and cyber capabilities
• 	A modernized army with strong artillery and air‑defense systems.
• 	A cyber force capable of supply‑chain compromises, ICS/SCADA disruption, and coordinated hybrid operations.
• 	State‑aligned hacker groups that operate with plausible deniability.

### Motivation for conflict
Varensk claims that parts of a neighboring region historically “belong” to it and argues that cultural and linguistic ties justify intervention. It frames its actions as “protecting ethnic Varenskers abroad.”
