# Tunneling Pivot — Student Guide

## Starting point

Begin from your **ops station**. Your assigned entry target is:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting address | `172.16.172.110` |

Confirm any classroom-specific remapping with your instructor before you begin.

## Objective

Get **your** image content displayed in **your two assigned locations** on the projected class screen.

How you present that content is up to you — one image, two different images, or content that changes over time are all acceptable if both of your locations show what you intend.

## Mission notes

- Investigate what is reachable from the starting address and what the projected display is asking for.
- Build whatever access paths you need so your content answers those requests. A path you open toward the web server and a path you expect the web server to open back to you are different. If one does not connect, try the other.
- When you gain remote execution, verify you are on the intended host before you dig further (`hostname`, identity, working path).
- Stay on assigned lab systems and do not interfere with other students' work, at least not until they have finished the assignment ;-)
- Keep image files on your ops station. Do not upload them to any target.

**Done when:** both of your assigned cells on the projected display show your intended image content.
