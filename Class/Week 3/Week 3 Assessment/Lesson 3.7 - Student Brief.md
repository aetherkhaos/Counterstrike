### **Week 3 Assessment: Enumeration, Gaining Access, and Situational Awareness Exercise**

**Date:** 4JUNE26  
**Duration:** Full training day (approximately 6–7 hours of work + two 10-minute team briefs)  
**Audience:** CounterStrike Operators (teams of 4)  
**Objective:** Validate your mastery of the **full military tactical planning process** (METT-TC → EMLCOA → CG/CV/Exp. Plan → SOM → FSP → TASKS), **enumeration, initial access, and situational awareness** by applying it against a real-world threat actor on a defended network. Your goal is to **generate viable options for pursuit, gain access to adversary infrastructure, and survey adversary terrain** in order to deny or degrade the adversary’s ability to collect intelligence.

You will use **all** of the Week 1 frameworks (tactical tasks, SALUTE/DRAW-D/OCOKA-W, EMLCOA, CG/CV analysis, MITRE ATT&CK, MITRE D3FEND, MITRE ENGAGE, and D3FEND CAD where appropriate). Additionally, you will **enumerate, exploit, and gain situational awareness on** adversary infrastructure and update your tactical planning process accordingly.

---

## Scenario Overview

You have been tasked by the commander to conduct a counterstrike operation from the **Lunovia Network** against **the Varenskian** nation-state adversary. The network is currently compromised and “bleeding” intelligence.

- **Mission (given):** On order, **Generate viable options for pursuit** in order to deny or degrade the adversary’s ability to collect intelligence. Upon gaining access to adversary infrastructure, reconnoiter the terrain IOT inform command decision making.
- **Tactical task orientation:** Primarily **enemy-oriented** (interdict/disrupt) with supporting terrain-oriented and friendly-oriented tasks as needed.

You will receive five key inputs at the start of the day: - EIS Group Seraph-3 - Seraph-3 Threat Hunt Report - Network Map - Hunt Notes - Tactical Planning Student Guide

## Assessment Structure (Two Phases)

The exercise is deliberately split into two phases to mirror the real planning timeline and allow for mid-course correction after commander feedback.

### **Phase 1: Begin the planning, Arrange Recon, Make Recon**

**Time allotted:** ~3 hours (including analysis and prep)  
**Deliverable:** Completed brief covering the entire input side of the planning process. Produce **one consolidated team brief** that includes:

- **METT-TC, EMLCOA, CG/CV**
- Mission analysis (explicit/implied tasks + purpose)
- Enemy analysis (SALUTE + DRAW-D)
- Troops & Fire Support Available
- Terrain & Weather analysis (Friendly OCOKA-W **and** Enemy OCOKA-W across all three layers of cyberspace)
- Time/Space/Logistics + Civilian Considerations (ASCOPE/PMESII as relevant)
- EMLCOA (one-paragraph enemy scheme of maneuver)
- Center of Gravity (CG)
- Critical Vulnerability (CV)
    
- **Exploitation Plan**
    
- Map the adversary’s key ATT&CK techniques (from your Navigator layer) to this scenario.
- For each major technique (or cluster), identify **paired D3FEND (D3) + ENGAGE (EAC)** options.
- Explicitly state for each option: goal (Expose/Affect/Elicit), expected effect on adversary CG/CV, risk, and authority required.
- Produce a **D3FEND CAD graph** (minimum: Agent node + 2–3 Attack nodes + 1 Artifact + 1 Condition + 1+ Countermeasure; use provenance and typed edges).
- **DECISION POINT:** Identify initial access vectors into adversary infrastructure. Brief the commander and await their decision before attempting access.
    
- **Scheme of Maneuver (SOM)**
    
- Phased plan that directly targets the CV.
- Describe how you will occupy/deny terrain across physical, logical, and persona layers.
- Address adversary actions on contact (withdraw, reinforce, etc.).
    
- **Fire Support Plan (FSP)**
    
- Identify rear-echelon / higher / adjacent support required (forensics, malware analysis, infrastructure, legal, intel, etc.).
- Deconfliction plan with other friendly cyber elements.
    
- **Tasks**
    
- Specific tactical tasks (with purpose “in order to…” statements) assigned to each team member/role.
- Reference the tactical task handout from Block 1 where applicable.
- Include measures of performance/effectiveness and supervision plan.
    
- **Include the specific MITRE ATT&CK T-Codes utilized to:**
    
    - Conduct Reconnaissance
    - Develop any resources

**Output format:** Use the Tactical Planning Process student handout templates. Be specific, evidence-based, and honest about assumptions/RFIs.

**10-Minute Team Brief (end of Phase 1):**  
Each team will deliver a **10-minute back-brief** (8 min presentation + 2 min Q&A) to the instructor cadre and the class. Focus on: - Your exploitation plan. **Do not speak in vague platitudes.** Provide concrete technical details (with evidence) about any plans to gain initial access. - Key assumptions and RFIs

## **Goal of brief:** Provide the Commander with options before you move into phase 2 and gaining access to adversary infrastructure.

### **Phase 2: Complete the Planning, Gain Access, Gain Situational Awareness**

**Time allotted:** ~2.5–3 hours **Deliverable:** Commander-ready execution products. Add to your Phase 1 brief:

- **Graphical Depiction of Operation**
- Using whatever tool you want, provide the commander with a visual of the operation that was conducted (give your brief over some kind of map).
    
- **Operation AAR**
    
- Include the specific MITRE ATT&CK T-Codes utilized to:
    - Gain initial access
    - Gain code execution
    - Privilege escalate
    - Any defense impairment or stealth techniques
    - Gain situational awareness
    - Any collection or exfiltration
    - C2/Interact with the target

**10-Minute Team Brief (end of Phase 2):**  
Each team delivers a **10-minute execution brief** (8 min + 2 min Q&A). Treat this as a formal commander’s brief. Include: - Your Exploitation Plan (highlight D3 + EAC pairings and CAD graph) - SOM overview (phases) - FSP and Tasks - Overall confidence in “stopping the bleeding” and why this plan will work

**Visual aid:** Project your CAD graph + any key slides/tables from your packet.

## Evaluation Criteria

Instructors will assess based on: - **Analytical rigor** — Use of doctrine (METT-TC, DRAW-D, OCOKA-W, EMLCOA, CG/CV) with evidence from the intel sheet and map. - **Framework integration** — Accurate mapping of ATT&CK → D3FEND/ENGAGE, realistic option pairing, and quality CAD graph. - **Feasibility & discipline** — Options respect authorities, risk, operational safety, and “do not spook the adversary” principles. - **Clarity & professionalism** — Briefs are concise, visual, and commander-focused; written products are clear and usable as hand-off artifacts. - **Teamwork** — Full participation; every member contributes visibly.
