# Framework-to-Tactical-Task Synthesis — Instructor Guide

## Objective and context

Teams close the loop from tactical tasks through ATT&CK, D3FEND, and ENGAGE, then back to a doctrinal task for each side of the option. Expected products and acceptable cells are in [`answer-key.md`](answer-key.md).

## Facilitation flow

1. Project the closed-loop figure and the mission line: deny or degrade FSB Center 18 collection from Network X in order to preserve friendly freedom of maneuver.
2. Walk one filled example row (valid accounts, script execution, or remote services) so teams see task, D3 option, ENGAGE option, goal, effect, and risk.
3. Give teams the five-log table. Some defend and engage cells are already started. The tactical-task cells are theirs.
4. Timebox the build, then back-brief one row per team.
5. Use the risk chart. Challenge any row marked extremely high without a probability and a consequence.

## Timing

About 45 minutes, including back-briefs. Protect the last minutes for the cleanup checks: SALUTE, DRAW-D, OCOKA-W, exploitation plan, sub-technique choice, risk bands, Navigator layer, and D3FEND graph.

## Common stalls

Teams copy a parent technique when the log supports a sub-technique. Teams treat every risk as high. Teams leave a tactical-task cell as a tool name.
