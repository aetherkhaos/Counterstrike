# DRAW-D Challenge — Instructor Answer Key

## Objective and starting context

Teams write DRAW-D for one actor from the SALUTE exercise and turn guesses into RFIs. Source: content guide slides 16, 17, and 30, and the student-deck DRAW-D definitions (defend, reinforce, attack, withdraw, delay).

## Expected product

Five DRAW-D lines, each with can / limit / condition where the report supports it, plus RFIs.

## Acceptable answers

For FSB Center 18:

- Defend and reinforce: resilient third-party services and possible handoff to more capable support. Redundant backdoors and scheduled tasks are the evidence.
- Attack: destructive action is possible and is not the observed mission. The observed mission is collection.
- Withdraw: full withdrawal is unlikely. Redundant and self-propagating access makes a clean exit hard. Do not treat "any anomaly" as this actor's withdraw condition unless the team cites evidence.
- Delay: obfuscation and volume of compromise slow defenders.

If the tasking were reconnoiter and the actor withdrew on any traffic anomaly, the scheme of maneuver would have to stay inside normal traffic. That is the lecture's contrast case, not the default for this actor.

## Grading checkpoints

- Accept: actor-specific lines and at least one RFI that names the missing fact.
- Do not accept: destructive EMLCOA solely because root access makes it possible.
