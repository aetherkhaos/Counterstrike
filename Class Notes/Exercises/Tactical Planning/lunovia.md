 A mid‑sized democratic state with a growing tech sector, strong national identity, and a defensive cyber posture.

### Core identity
Lunovia is a nation of 32 million people located along Varensk’s southwestern border. It has a parliamentary democracy, a diversified economy, and a rapidly expanding digital infrastructure. Its population is younger, more urban, and more globally connected than Varensk’s.

### Geography
Lunovia is a mid‑sized democratic state with a mix of fertile plains, river valleys, and modern urban centers.
- Capital: Velystok
- Population: ~32 million
- Terrain: Rolling plains, agricultural heartland, dense forests in the north
- Borders: Varensk (NE), two friendly democracies (W/S)
- Key geographic features:
	- The Lunovian Corridor: A flat, open region vulnerable to mechanized invasion.
	- The Vel River: Runs through the capital and powers hydroelectric plants.
	- The Western Highlands: Natural defensive terrain.
- Strategic value:
	- Major tech sector and digital‑government infrastructure.
	- Key transit route for regional energy pipelines.

### Political Systems
A parliamentary democracy with strong civil institutions.
- Government type: Parliamentary republic
- Prime Minister: Alina Veskova
- Power structure:
	- Multiparty coalition government
	- Independent judiciary
	- Free press and active civil society
	- Ideology: Liberal democracy, economic modernization, integration with international alliances
- Foreign policy:
	- Strengthen ties with Western democracies
	- Resist Varenskian influence
	- Build cyber resilience and digital sovereignty

### Strategic posture
• 	Seeks independence, integration with international institutions, and economic modernization.
• 	Invests heavily in defensive cyber capabilities, especially critical‑infrastructure protection.
• 	Relies on partnerships with foreign cybersecurity firms and allied intelligence sharing.
• 	Maintains a smaller but professional military with strong territorial defense doctrine.

### Military and cyber capabilities
• 	A compact army focused on mobility, drones, and asymmetric defense.
• 	A national cyber defense agency that protects government networks and key industries.
• 	Volunteer cyber auxiliaries that can surge during crises.
• 	Heavy reliance on cloud services and digital government platforms—making it vulnerable to disruption.

### Motivation for conflict
Lunovia rejects Varensk’s territorial claims and has been moving politically and economically toward alliances that Varensk views as hostile. Border skirmishes, cyber intrusions, and political interference have escalated tensions.

### Lunovia Cyber Defense Structure
1. National Cyber Defense Agency (NCDA)
	1. Protects government networks
	2. Coordinates incident response
	3. Runs national CERT
2. Digital Infrastructure Protection Corps (DIPC)
	1. Guards energy, telecom, and transportation sectors
	2. Works with private industry
	3. Cyber Auxiliary Reserve
3. Volunteer experts activated during crises
	1. Supports threat hunting and rapid patching
4. Foreign Cyber Partnerships
	1. Intelligence sharing with allied democracies
	2. Joint exercises and red‑team exchanges
