### 4.3 Lesson summary

- The course uses one fictional setting so every block shares a common target, terrain, and adversary without classification problems.
- **Varensk** is the authoritarian adversary: three cyber directorates (D7, EIS, ISB) and named units with C2 assignments and real-world analogues (*Amber Tide* / Gamaredon; *Seraph-3* / Cozy Bear).
- **Lunovia** is the defender: NCDA, DIPC, foreign partnerships, and heavy cloud reliance. Blue team = Lunovia; DCO-RA out of blue-space = a Lunovian unit.
- The course network diagram is the terrain for planning, tradecraft, on-target, and agentic work.

- **Ask (Q):** Why is the scenario fictional?
- **Expected Response (A):** Classification / attribution safety, and a common target-terrain-adversary for every block.
- **Ask (Q):** Name Varensk's three cyber directorates and one unit you will see again.

- **Expected Response (A):** Steer toward cloud reliance, NCDA/DIPC structure, Lunovian Corridor / Vel River / Western Highlands, and the course network diagram as terrain.
